🏛️ Babylonian Origins

Trigonometry originated from ancient Babylon, around 1800 BCE.

  • Track celestial bodies and stars
  • Calculate distances without measurement
  • Develop astronomy and calendar systems

🔢 Base-60 (Sexagesimal) System

Why is 60 special? It divides evenly by many numbers!

60 is divisible by:

60 ÷ 1 = 60 ✓
60 ÷ 2 = 30 ✓
60 ÷ 3 = 20 ✓
60 ÷ 4 = 15 ✓
60 ÷ 5 = 12 ✓
60 ÷ 6 = 10 ✓
60 ÷ 10 = 6 ✓

No decimals needed! Easy to measure anything.
